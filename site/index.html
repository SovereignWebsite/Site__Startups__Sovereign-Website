How each one was made

  1. The interviewAn AI interview about five things, ending in a one-page brief. This step is “a design that is still being built”.
  2. The materialThe owner supplies what they have: a zip, an email, links or files.
  3. The team buildsAgents with job descriptions and a behaviour policy build it, each signing its work.
  4. Five designsThe owner picks one design; the others are kept.
  5. The hand-overThe site is handed over in a repository the owner controls.
  6. The first changeThe milestone is the owner's first change, made by talking to their own AI.

Sources: 7, 11

What Sovereign Website is

A team of AI agents builds a website, shows five designs and hands it over. The files, history and address belong to the owner, who can move it anywhere.

It comes in two forms: an open pattern that anyone can use for free, the way anyone can install WordPress, and a hosted version that is being prepared.

“Sovereign” here means owning the site and being able to move it. The sites say plainly that this “is not a legal or certification claim”.

Status, as the company states it: “Nothing is sold yet. The hosted service under this name, and the pattern's own repository, do not exist yet.”

What a visitor can do today: take the “one-minute self-check” on the company's maturity model, which places a website from level 0, “rented”, to level 5, “sovereign and proven”.
