Subject

Sovereign Website

Websites built by agents, owned by you
Originsovereignwebsite.ai
Sheet checked2026-11-20

Specification

A team of AI agents builds a static website, shows the owner five designs and hands it over. The files, the history and the address belong to the owner, who can move it anywhere and change it by talking to their own AI.

A team of AI agents builds a website, shows five designs and hands it over. The files, history and address belong to the owner, who can move it anywhere.1

It comes in two forms: an open pattern that anyone can use for free, the way anyone can install WordPress, and a hosted version that is being prepared.2 3

"Sovereign" here means owning the site and being able to move it. The sites say plainly that this "is not a legal or certification claim".1 4

Status, as the company states it: "Nothing is sold yet. The hosted service under this name, and the pattern's own repository, do not exist yet."1

Stated purpose: “Your website. Built by agents, owned by you.” (The headline on sovereignwebsite.ai)1

Intended users

User 1Small businesses such as "a florist, a bakery or a surf school", who need not know the stack underneath.1
User 2People moving off Wix, Squarespace or WordPress.11
User 3Partners who would resell it: freelancers, small consultancies and larger firms (a draft programme).12
User 4Developers and anyone who wants to use the open pattern themselves.6

Process

  1. The interviewAn AI interview about five things, ending in a one-page brief. This step is "a design that is still being built".7
  2. The materialThe owner supplies what they have: a zip, an email, links or files.7
  3. The team buildsAgents with job descriptions and a behaviour policy build it, each signing its work.7 13
  4. Five designsThe owner picks one design; the others are kept.7
  5. The hand-overThe site is handed over in a repository the owner controls.7
  6. The first changeThe milestone is the owner's first change, made by talking to their own AI.7

Components

sovereignwebsite.aiThe brand and the hosted offer.1
sovereignwebsite.orgThe principles and the open pattern, "as wordpress.org is to WordPress".2 6
sovereignwebsite.siteThe address for hosted sites. It says: "There are no hosted sites here yet."5
The HubThe showroom of live sites.16
The VaultOpens a private site sealed in an encrypted sgit vault, in the browser.17
