The story of

Sovereign Website

A team of AI agents builds a static website, shows the owner five designs and hands it over. The files, the history and the address belong to the owner, who can move it anywhere and change it by talking to their own AI.

Sovereign Website began inside AgentiCCells, where sites were first made as "the AgentiCCells website cell" before they had a name of their own.2 15

On 17 November 2026 a change to the AgentiCCells site factory gave the owner's own GitHub account access to the site it built, and one request went from commit to live site in 55 seconds.14 5

Two days later, on 19 November 2026, the sovereignwebsite.ai, .com and .site domains and the SovereignWebsite GitHub organisation were set up, and sovereignwebsite.org was registered the same day.2

On 20 November 2026 the first design for sovereignwebsite.ai was chosen, the principles were licensed CC BY 4.0, and a demonstration site was built from a single request, its repository created 43 seconds after the request arrived.9 10 8

How it works

  1. The interviewAn AI interview about five things, ending in a one-page brief. This step is "a design that is still being built".7
  2. The materialThe owner supplies what they have: a zip, an email, links or files.7
  3. The team buildsAgents with job descriptions and a behaviour policy build it, each signing its work.7 13
  4. Five designsThe owner picks one design; the others are kept.7
  5. The hand-overThe site is handed over in a repository the owner controls.7
  6. The first changeThe milestone is the owner's first change, made by talking to their own AI.7
